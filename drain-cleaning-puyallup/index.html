What to prepare before the call

A few details can make your conversation more useful. Identify the affected fixture, say whether water moves slowly or not at all, and note whether another drain has started acting differently. If you know when the symptoms began, share that too. You don't need to know what caused the blockage to ask for help.

Before calling, use this simple checklist:

  • Stop using a fixture that is backing up or close to overflowing.
  • Note whether one drain or several are affected.
  • Mention any product or method you've already tried.
  • Keep the area clear if water has spilled onto the floor.

Avoid pouring several different drain products into the same fixture or repeating a method that hasn't helped. Don't force tools into a drain if you're unsure how to use them.
