A short checklist before you book

Taking a few minutes to prepare can make the first conversation more focused. Keep these points in mind:

  • Record when it began and whether it has changed.
  • Say if it is affecting normal use of the plumbing.
  • Mention whether you’re requesting maintenance or help with a repair.
  • Write down questions you want answered before service.

Try not to assume that a symptom has only one possible cause. Another mistake is waiting to mention a concern because it seems intermittent. Tell us what you’ve seen, even if it doesn’t happen every time, and let the conversation determine what information is useful next.
